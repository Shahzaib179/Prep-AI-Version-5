# Prep AI v4.8 – quizzes from your own documents and PDF assignments

No new packages. Database changes (migration 9) apply automatically on start.

## 1. Tutor: create a quiz from your own document
Tutor Dashboard → **Create Quiz** → *Where should the questions come from?* → **My own document**.
- Upload up to 5 files (PDF, DOCX, TXT or MD, 15 MB each). Pick a subject (or **Other** and type one), difficulty and number of questions (5–50).
- Leave **Chapter / Topic** empty to cover the whole document, or type a chapter to focus on it.
- Questions are written only from your document. They are spread over the whole file (the generator works in rounds of 10, each round gets different passages), duplicates are removed, and you can edit them before publishing (Publish tab), save them to your question bank, or use them in a live quiz.
- Not supported: scanned documents (photos of pages). The app tells you when a file has no readable text. Very long documents (about 400 pages of text) must be split.
- The knowledge-base option works exactly as before.

## 2. PDF assignments (Tutor Dashboard → Classes → New assignment → PDF assignment)
Tutor: choose the class, give a title and instructions, upload the assignment PDF (up to 8 MB), optionally set total marks, a deadline, and whether late uploads are allowed.

Student (**My Classes**): sees the assignment with its deadline, downloads the PDF, uploads the solution as a PDF (up to 8 MB), can replace it until the deadline, and later sees marks and feedback.

Deadline rule (checked on the server at the moment of upload, not in the browser):
- before the due time: upload or replace freely;
- after it: refused, unless the tutor allowed late uploads; then the solution is accepted and flagged **late**;
- once the tutor has marked a solution it is locked; **Re-open** lets the student upload a corrected one (if the deadline still allows).

Tutor review: status table (submitted, late, not submitted), a list of students with no solution, open any solution, enter marks (limited to the total) and written feedback, download all solutions as one ZIP, download the CSV report, change the deadline, remove the assignment (this also deletes its files).

Quiz assignments work as before, side by side with PDF ones. The sidebar badge counts both.

Security: a student can only download assignments of their own classes and their own solution; a tutor only the files of their own classes; every download checks this again. Uploads must be real, openable, non-encrypted PDFs (the file signature and structure are checked, not just the name).

## Storage
Files are stored in the database (table `stored_files`) so they survive redeploys on any host. Supabase's free plan has 500 MB: a typical homework PDF is 0.1–2 MB, so this is fine for a class; deleting an assignment deletes its files. If you expect thousands of large scans, move to Supabase Storage later.
